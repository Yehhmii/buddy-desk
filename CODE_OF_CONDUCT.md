# Code of Conduct

## Our promise

We want taking part in this project to be a good experience for everyone, whatever their background, experience level, identity, or how they happen to look or sound. People should feel safe asking questions and making mistakes.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully. Critique the work, not the person.
- Respect other people's time. Reviews and replies come from volunteers.
- Keep discussion on topic and useful.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory jokes or language.
- Personal attacks, doxxing, or sharing someone's private information.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions, or repeated spam.
- Anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and remove people from the project when behaviour breaks these rules. Report problems privately to the maintainers at **francisco@pensavox.com**. Reports are handled in confidence, and reporters will not face retaliation.

This code is adapted from the ideas in the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
